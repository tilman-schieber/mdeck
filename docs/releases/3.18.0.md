## Hide a slide without deleting it

A slide can now stay in the deck without being shown: `hidden: true` in its settings, or the eye beside it in the editor's slide list. The deck, the presenter view, the audience window, the reader and PDFs skip it, and the page numbers count without it; the editor still shows it, greyed out. Built files, files from `mdeck send` and PDFs keep nothing of a hidden slide: not its words, pictures, drawings or answers.

## Moved strokes reach the audience window

Strokes moved with the lasso on a paired iPad now reach the audience window: a large move no longer stays behind in the old place. Live ink goes to the stage room in smaller requests, which also suits servers from before this release, and the audience window follows the saved drawings while `mdeck run` runs, so anything it missed live is put right with the next save.
